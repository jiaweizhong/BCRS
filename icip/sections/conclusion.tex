\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented DES-ESOD, a recall-oriented small-object detector built around a
dual-evidence, coverage-supervised region selector that targets the specific
failure mode objectness-only selective-computation detectors leave open:
silently dropping genuinely tiny, semantically weak objects. On UAVDT, DES-ESOD
improves mAP, total recall, and Very~Tiny recall over
an objectness-only baseline at a larger but not yet budget-matched retained
area; a matched-Occupy comparison and the pending TinyPersonV2 cross-domain
result are the two remaining pieces needed for a complete, honest claim of
generalization.
