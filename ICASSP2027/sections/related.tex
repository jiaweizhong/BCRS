\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Small Object Detection in Aerial Imagery}
\label{ssec:related_sod}

Detecting micro targets in high-resolution aerial and maritime vision is challenged by extreme scale disparity, low contrast, and massive background areas~\cite{VisDrone,UAVDT,TinyPerson,SeaDronesSee}. Classical solutions employ density-guided patch cropping (e.g., ClusDet~\cite{ClusDet}, DMNet~\cite{DMNet}, UFPMP-Det~\cite{UFPMP}) or coarse-to-fine slicing (e.g., ResBiDet~\cite{ResBiDet}), which incur redundant feature extraction and complex boundary stitching. Other works explore specialized metrics, such as Normalized Gaussian Wasserstein Distance (NWD)~\cite{NWD}, RFLA~\cite{RFLA}, and scale-aware balancing (SSABNet)~\cite{SSABNet}. However, dense detectors uniformly evaluate all spatial locations, leaving background redundancy unresolved.

\subsection{Selective and Dynamic Computation}
\label{ssec:related_selective}

Dynamic computation reduces arithmetic overhead by evaluating only prospective foreground regions. AutoFocus~\cite{AutoFocus} predicts focus pixels for multi-scale inference, QueryDet~\cite{QueryDet} accelerates feature pyramids via cascaded sparse queries, and CEASC~\cite{CEASC} designs adaptive activation masks. ESOD~\cite{ESOD} routes foreground patches (AdaSlicer) after the stem. Nonetheless, existing selectors rely strictly on single semantic activations and independent BCE loss, making them prone to early-stage pruning. In contrast, HESOD combines dual-evidence routing with joint soft-coverage optimization.

\subsection{Spectral Saliency and Frequency Representations}
\label{ssec:related_spectral}

Frequency-domain cues provide rich spatial structural priors. SET~\cite{SET} demonstrated that micro objects preserve distinct high-frequency gradients even when semantic responses fade, though it applies dense frequency operations across feature pyramids. HESOD repurposes spectral saliency as an ultra-lightweight, channel-pooled routing signal to ensure recall-safe selection at negligible overhead.
