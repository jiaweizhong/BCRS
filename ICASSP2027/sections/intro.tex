\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

High-resolution aerial and maritime vision is essential for remote surveillance, environmental monitoring, and search-and-rescue~\cite{VisDrone,UAVDT,TinyPerson}. In these settings, micro targets typically occupy fewer than $16\times 16$ pixels amidst vast background areas ($>70\text{--}80\%$), making dense uniform convolution computationally prohibitive on edge platforms~\cite{ESOD,AutoFocus}.

Spatial selective computation mitigates arithmetic redundancy by routing processing strictly to candidate foreground regions~\cite{QueryDet,CEASC,ESOD}. However, existing selectors exhibit two critical vulnerabilities: (i) \emph{Evidence fragility}: relying solely on deep semantic activations leads to severe feature collapse for micro targets after stem downsampling, causing irrecoverable false-negative pruning under asymmetric selection cost~\cite{SET,ESOD}; and (ii) \emph{Coverage deficit}: standard per-pixel binary cross-entropy optimizes grid cells independently, failing to guarantee joint multi-cell coverage over individual instances.

As illustrated in Fig.~\ref{fig:teaser}, faint micro targets (e.g., compact vehicles or distant surfers) easily suffer severe coarse-stage pruning in existing single-evidence selectors (e.g., ESOD~\cite{ESOD}) due to degraded semantic contrast. To resolve these challenges, we propose \textbf{HESOD} (\textbf{H}igh-Resolution \textbf{E}fficient \textbf{S}mall \textbf{O}bject \textbf{D}etection), a dual-evidence selective framework that couples high-frequency spectral gradients with semantic objectness and soft-coverage optimization. More specifically, our key contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}\setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}\setlength{\parskip}{0pt}\setlength{\topsep}{1pt}
    \item \textbf{Dual-Evidence Spatial Selector}: We couple high-level semantic objectness with channel-pooled spectral saliency at $\mathcal{O}(1)$ spatial complexity, reliably preserving degraded micro targets.
    \item \textbf{Object-Level Soft-Coverage Loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cover}}$)}: We formulate a differentiable joint-coverage objective to suppress premature false-negative pruning under asymmetric selection cost.
    \item \textbf{Scale-Aware Edge-Efficient Head}: We integrate an inverted residual decoupled head with scale-aware Wasserstein regression to simultaneously reduce inference overhead and enhance micro-target localization.
\end{enumerate}
