\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented HESOD, uniting semantic objectness with constant-width spectral saliency via monotonic max-fusion to address early semantic collapse on micro targets. An object-level soft-coverage loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cover}}$) optimizes instance coverage under asymmetric pruning risk, while a lightweight ISPP head amortizes downstream overhead without sacrificing selector gains. On UAVDT and SeaPerson, HESOD improves $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ by 0.9\,pp and 2.3\,pp (total recall +3.66\,pp and +3.33\,pp), cuts parameters by \textgreater27\%, and achieves 106.1/80.6 FPS on an RTX 5090. Dual-evidence routing reaches 0.940/0.991 BPR, substantially reducing routing-stage misses and shifting the remaining bottleneck to downstream regression. These results confirm that spectral structural priors enable accuracy-efficiency co-design for resource-efficient deployment.
