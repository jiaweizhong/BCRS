\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we presented HESOD, an efficient dual-evidence selective framework for small object detection in high-resolution aerial and maritime vision. By coupling semantic objectness with channel-pooled spectral saliency at $\mathcal{O}(1)$ complexity under an evidence preservation principle and optimizing joint instance coverage via a soft-coverage loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cover}}$), HESOD prevents premature false-negative pruning on micro targets. Integrated with an inverted residual partial-convolution head (ISPP) and scale-aware regression (SABL), HESOD achieves +0.7\,pp $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ (+3.13\,pp recall) on UAVDT and +2.4\,pp $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ on SeaPerson over baseline ESOD, while slashing parameters by 27.6\% with negligible FLOPs overhead.

Future work will investigate adaptive spatial threshold scheduling and extend dual-evidence selective routing to multimodal aerial perception platforms.
