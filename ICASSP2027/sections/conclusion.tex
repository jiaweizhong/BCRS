\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented HESOD, uniting semantic objectness with spectral saliency via monotonic max-fusion to address early semantic collapse on micro targets, with an object-level soft-coverage loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cover}}$) and a lightweight decoupled head (ISPP) recovering downstream overhead. On UAVDT and SeaPerson, HESOD improves $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ by 0.9\,pp and 2.3\,pp (total recall +3.66\,pp and +3.33\,pp), cuts parameters by $>27\%$, and achieves 106.1/80.6 FPS on an RTX 5090. Dual-evidence routing reaches 0.940/0.991 BPR and cuts selector false negatives to as low as 19.2\% on SeaPerson, shifting the remaining bottleneck to downstream regression refinement and enabling resource-efficient deployment.
