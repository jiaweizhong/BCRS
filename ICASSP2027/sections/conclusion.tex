\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented HESOD, an efficient dual-evidence framework for high-resolution small object detection under selective computation. By uniting semantic objectness with constant-width spectral saliency via monotonic max-fusion, HESOD resolves the fundamental dilemma of early semantic collapse on micro targets. An object-level soft-coverage loss ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cover}}$) directly optimizes instance coverage under asymmetric pruning risks, while an inverted residual decoupled head (ISPP) amortizes downstream evaluation overhead. On UAVDT and SeaPerson, HESOD improves $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ by 0.9\,pp and 2.3\,pp (total recall +3.66\,pp and +3.33\,pp) while cutting parameters by $>27\%$. These results confirm that spectral structural priors can effectively safeguard coarse-stage routing, establishing a practical accuracy-efficiency co-design for edge drone platforms.
