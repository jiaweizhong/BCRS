\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented HESOD, an efficient dual-evidence framework for high-resolution small object detection. Uniting semantic-spectral routing, monotonic evidence preservation, and soft coverage ($\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cover}}$), HESOD prevents premature pruning of micro targets. Coupled with an ISPP decoupled head, HESOD boosts $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ by 0.9\,pp and 2.3\,pp (+3.66\,pp and +3.33\,pp total recall) on UAVDT and SeaPerson while compressing parameters by $>27\%$, achieving a practical accuracy-efficiency co-design for resource-constrained deployment.
