\subsection{Experiment Settings}
\label{ssec:settings}

We evaluate HESOD on two demanding high-resolution small-object benchmarks: \textbf{UAVDT}~\cite{UAVDT} ($1280 \times 1280\text{ px}$, urban vehicle surveillance across 3 classes) and \textbf{SeaPerson}~\cite{TinyPerson,SeaDronesSee} ($2048 \times 2048\text{ px}$, maritime search-and-rescue with $>85\%$ micro targets $<32\text{ px}$). Both benchmarks evaluate full-resolution images without artificial pre-cropping.

All models are implemented in PyTorch using a YOLOv5m base detector and trained with SGD for 50 epochs following standard selective protocols~\cite{ESOD}. Detection accuracy is assessed via COCO-style $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ and scale-stratified recall across pixel area bins: \emph{Very Tiny} ($<16^2$), \emph{Tiny} ($16^2\text{--}32^2$), and \emph{Small} ($32^2\text{--}96^2$). Computational efficiency is measured via parameter count, GFLOPs, FPS (batch size 1), and Bounding Patch Recall (BPR).

\textbf{Comparison with Dense Baselines.} As shown in Table~\ref{tab:main_results}, classical dense detectors incur prohibitive arithmetic overhead on multi-megapixel vision grids due to uniform convolution. Compared to Faster R-CNN and RetinaNet on SeaPerson ($2048\times 2048$), HESOD delivers substantial accuracy gains of \textbf{+22.3\,pp} and \textbf{+30.1\,pp} $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$, while slashing GFLOPs by \textbf{7.4$\times$} and \textbf{4.5$\times$} at \textbf{80.7 FPS}, confirming that selective computation is essential for edge deployment.

\textbf{Comparison with Selective Baseline (ESOD).} Against ESOD, HESOD achieves clear accuracy, recall, and parameter advantages across both benchmarks. On SeaPerson, HESOD advances $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ by \textbf{+2.4\,pp} (0.774 vs. 0.750) and $\mathrm{mAP@.5:.95}$ by \textbf{+0.6\,pp}, while cutting parameters by \textbf{27.6\%} (25.9M vs. 35.8M) at 80.7 FPS. On UAVDT ($1280\times 1280$), HESOD enhances overall instance recovery by \textbf{+3.77\,pp} (88.94\% vs. 85.17\%, recovering +14,109 targets) with consistent recall gains across all vehicle classes (\emph{car}: +3.70\,pp, \emph{truck}: +2.85\,pp, \emph{bus}: +7.96\,pp), establishing a superior accuracy-efficiency Pareto frontier.

\textbf{Dual Evidence and Soft-Coverage Optimization.} Table~\ref{tab:ablation} isolates individual module contributions. On SeaPerson, replacing standard BCE with object-level soft-coverage loss $\mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{cover}}$ (Arm 1 $\to$ 2) yields an immediate \textbf{+1.9\,pp} $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ boost and raises Bounding Patch Recall (BPR) to \textbf{0.991}, verifying that joint coverage prevents premature selector pruning. Furthermore, channel-pooled spectral routing (Arm 4) preserves unpooled accuracy (Arm 3) at $\mathcal{O}(1)$ spatial complexity, while dual-evidence fusion (Arm 5) attains \textbf{0.772} $\mathrm{mAP@.5}$ and rescues +631 micro targets ($<16^2\text{ px}$).

\textbf{Edge Decoupled Head and Localization Loss.} Adopting the inverted residual ISPP head (Arm 7) cuts compute by \textbf{22.6\%} (217.6 vs. 281.2 GFLOPs) and trims parameters by \textbf{27.6\%}, achieving \textbf{0.328} $\mathrm{mAP@.5:.95}$. Adding scale-aware Wasserstein regression (SABL, Arm 8) further improves sub-16px localization, pushing \emph{Very Tiny} recall to a peak \textbf{77.14\%} (+2,560 targets over baseline), overall recall to \textbf{88.11\%}, and runtime compute to \textbf{209.0 GFLOPs} at \textbf{80.7 FPS}. Full HESOD thus attains the optimal Pareto trade-off.

\textbf{Error Bottleneck Diagnosis.} Stratified breakdown confirms that target recovery is strongest on \emph{Tiny} (+4.81\,pp) and \emph{Very Tiny} (+3.11\,pp) strata. Error diagnosis reveals that dual routing and coverage optimization reduce selector-dropped false negatives from $25.6\%$ in baseline ESOD to \textbf{$19.2\%$}, shifting the remaining bottleneck to downstream refinement.

\textbf{Qualitative Comparison.} As shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:qualitative} on UAVDT and SeaPerson, baseline ESOD misses weak-contrast micro targets due to semantic degradation, whereas HESOD's dual-evidence routing and scale-aware regression accurately localize all tiny instances without false alarms.
